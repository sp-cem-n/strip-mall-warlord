# What the player actually does (v0.5)

**In one line:** you grow a dying strip mall one storefront at a time, and the crew those shops send out fights its own way across the parking lots.

## Moment to moment (30 seconds at a time)
Watch the lot. Twenty-odd people in ranks (tanks in front, buffers at the back) trade hits with security, consultants and a Developer. The badge over an enemy is who they're weak to (+25%). The tag next to the fight is the enemy's **tell**, *tanky* or *hard*. You do three things:
- **Call the play:** Rush beats tanky, Dig In beats hard. On a loss, the receipt says which call would have helped.
- **Overtime:** two minutes of rent buys +25% for the next three fights, to break a boss now.
- **Push or fall back:** use ◂ / ▸ and the wins-to-move-up counter. Grab a walk-in when one shows up.

## Session to session (a few minutes, a few times a day)
Open the game. The title shows what happened while you were away. Spend the $ (the ▲ line on each card says what the next renovation buys), spend Goodwill on a role track and close the tab. The Security Office keeps the crew pushing all night.

## After the ending
At dawn you choose to keep the lights on (farm) or pave Route 9 (restart with +2% per asphalt) and drive north to **the Bypass**. That's a second crew with new shops and Equity bought with Route 9 Goodwill. Both crews always fight, and the tabs only pick which one you manage. The loop is to bank Goodwill, pave, re-climb Route 9 faster, and bank again until the Galleria falls.

## Where it's idle
Rent, fights, advancing (via Security) and both crews run on their own, offline too (8h cap). No decision is on a timer, and being away never costs anything. Attention makes it faster, not possible.

## Where the joy comes from
1. **The call that lands.** A boss that walled you falls on Dig In.
2. **The plaza filling up.** Whitewashed windows become lit signs, and bosses show up in the back office.
3. **The text.** "Oversees eleven stores and a golf cart."
4. **The reveal and the loop.** Dawn, the Bypass, and the second Route 9 run blowing through lots that took hours.
